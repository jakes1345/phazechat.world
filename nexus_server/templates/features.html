Features

Everything in Phaze, and exactly where it works.

Every feature on this page ships today. Where something only works on one platform, or only with a particular theme, the badge under it says so.

Themes decide features. In the web app, each era theme shows the features classic Skype had at the time. The default, Phaze 5, covers everyday chat and calls. Switch to Phaze 6+ in Settings → Appearance for Spaces, reactions, read receipts, live streams and remote sessions.

Messaging

Chat that behaves the way you expect.

One-to-one DMs, group chats and the small things that make a conversation feel alive.

Encrypted direct messages

DMs are encrypted on your device with NaCl box (X25519 and XSalsa20-Poly1305) once your app and your friend’s have exchanged keys. The lock in the message box shows it’s on. Anything sent before that goes as plain text.

End-to-end encryptedWebAndroid

Group chats

Start a group from your contacts. The creator can add and remove people and rename the chat. The chat header tells everyone that groups are not end-to-end encrypted.

Not end-to-end encryptedWebAndroid

Edits, deletes, reactions, receipts

Fix a typo or take a message back, and see when your friend is typing. Reactions, read receipts and pinned messages appear with the Phaze 6+ theme.

WebAndroid

Files up to 25 MB

Send photos, audio, video, PDFs and archives. The message that carries the link is encrypted in a DM, but the file itself is stored on the server unencrypted under a random, unguessable name.

WebAndroid

Voice messages

Tap the mic button to record a quick voice note when typing won’t do, and play it back right in the conversation.

Web

Stories

Share a photo or short video that disappears after 24 hours. Friends see it in the row of story rings at the top of their chat list.

Phaze 5 & 6+ themesNot end-to-end encrypted
Contacts & presence

The contact list is the product.

This is the part of classic Skype people miss most, so it’s the part Phaze takes most seriously.

Friends, groups and blocking

Send and accept friend requests, sort people into contact groups, and block anyone you don’t want to hear from.

WebAndroid

Presence and mood messages

Online, away, do not disturb or invisible, with a mood message under your name, shown next to everyone in your list.

WebAndroid

Skype import

Upload the .zip export from Skype (up to 100 MB). Phaze imports the text of your conversations and lists your old contacts so you can find or invite them. How it works.

WebAndroid
Calls & screens

Talk, show, and share.

Calls ring every device you’re signed in on. Here’s how each kind of session actually connects.

Voice and video calls

Calls between friends open a room on the public Jitsi Meet service (meet.jit.si). On Android the call opens in the Jitsi Meet call screen. Jitsi’s servers carry the audio and video, so calls are not covered by Phaze’s end-to-end encryption.

Not end-to-end encryptedWebAndroid

Screen sharing in calls

Share your screen during a web call using the Jitsi call controls. Screen sharing isn’t available in the Android app.

Web

Live streams

Broadcast your camera or screen to other signed-in Phaze users. Video goes over direct WebRTC connections, relayed through Phaze’s TURN server when a direct path isn’t possible.

WebPhaze 6+ theme

Remote screen sessions

Share your screen with one person using a 6-digit code. They see a live pointer overlay and you can swap files over the connection. It’s for showing and guiding: they can’t control your mouse or keyboard.

WebPhaze 6+ theme
Spaces

Community servers for your group.

Spaces are for friend groups, clubs and communities that outgrow a group chat.

Text channels

Organise a Space into channels, with pinned messages, reactions and edits. Channel messages are deleted after 30 days by default, and are readable by the server.

WebPhaze 6+ themeNot end-to-end encrypted

Voice channels

Drop into a voice channel and talk. Like calls, voice channels run in a Jitsi Meet room.

WebPhaze 6+ theme

Shared whiteboard

Sketch out a plan together on a whiteboard everyone in the channel can draw on.

WebPhaze 6+ theme
Accounts & security

Locked down by default.

Your account is protected even if someone learns your password.

Verified sign-up

You sign up with a username, password and email address, confirmed with a code. No phone number. Disposable email addresses are refused.

WebAndroid

Two-factor and device approval

Turn on two-factor codes from any authenticator app. Every new device has to be approved from one that’s already signed in, or with a code sent to your email.

WebAndroid

QR sign-in and sessions

Sign in on a new screen by scanning a QR code. See every active session and sign out the ones you don’t recognise.

WebAndroid

Key backup with a PIN

Your encryption keys can be backed up encrypted with a PIN (PBKDF2-SHA256, 200,000 iterations, AES-256-GCM). Restore them on a new device to keep reading your encrypted history.

Web

Export or delete your data

Download a JSON copy of your account data, or delete your account, from Settings at any time.

Web

Push notifications

Get messages while the app is closed: web push in the browser, and Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android.

WebAndroid
Look & platforms

Classic on the outside, open underneath.

Pick the era you remember, then use Phaze wherever you are.

Six era themes

Phaze 1 to Phaze 6+ are modelled on classic Skype releases from 2007 to 2018: XP Luna, Vista Aero, 2010, Metro, 2014 Aero blue and 2018 Fluent. See them side by side.

Emoticons

A full set of emoticons drawn for Phaze, with the classic text shortcuts like :) and (y).

Web, Android and desktop

One account everywhere. The web app works in any modern browser, Android installs from an APK, and Windows and Linux desktop apps are in beta. Downloads.

WebAndroidDesktop

Self-hostable

Run your own Phaze server from the published Docker image. Your community, your hardware, your rules.

Open source

Free, with no ads

No subscriptions, no ads, no data sold. Inviting a friend earns cosmetic rewards; nothing is for sale. Pricing.
