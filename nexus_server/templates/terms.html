1. What Phaze is

Phaze is a messaging service provided “as is” by the Phaze project maintainers. The apps connect to a server that relays and stores messages. One-to-one DMs are end-to-end encrypted once both apps have exchanged keys; group chats, Spaces, stories and calls are not (see Security). The software is open source, and the public server at phazechat.world is run on a best-effort basis with no guarantee of availability.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 13 years old, or older where your local law requires.
  • Sign-up needs a username, a password and an email address you verify. You’re responsible for keeping your password and devices secure.
  • One person per account. Automated sign-ups and bulk registration aren’t allowed. Bots need the operator’s permission.

3. Acceptable use

Don’t use Phaze to:

  • Harass, threaten, impersonate or stalk anyone.
  • Share child sexual abuse material. Reports of CSAM lead to immediate termination and referral to the appropriate authorities.
  • Distribute malware or phishing, or attempt to break into accounts or the service.
  • Send spam or unsolicited bulk messages.
  • Evade blocks, bans, rate limits or other abuse protections.
  • Break the law or infringe other people’s rights.

4. Moderation

Moderation depends on what we can see. Group chats, Spaces channels and stories are stored in readable form, so administrators can review them, remove content and act on accounts, including in response to reports. End-to-end encrypted DMs can’t be read by us; for those we rely on reports through the in-app Report option, where the reporter can include the messages in question. Space owners can also manage their own Spaces.

5. Your content

You own what you send. You give us permission to store, relay and display it as needed to run the service for you and the people you share it with. You’re responsible for having the right to share it.

6. Changes and termination

  • We may suspend or terminate accounts that break these terms, with or without notice.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings.
  • If the public server is going to shut down or move, we’ll give at least 14 days’ notice on the support page and in the app. Because the software is open source, anyone can run their own server.

7. No warranty and liability

Phaze is provided without warranties of any kind, express or implied. To the extent the law allows, the maintainers are not liable for lost data, lost messages, failed calls or any indirect damages arising from using the service.

8. Open source

The Phaze apps and server are released under the MIT License. Source: github.com/jakes1345/phazechat.world. The license covers the code; these terms cover your use of the hosted service.

9. Contact

Questions: hello@phazechat.world. Abuse reports: abuse@phazechat.world. Security issues: report privately.
