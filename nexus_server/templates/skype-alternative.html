Skype alternative

Skype is gone. The way it felt doesn’t have to be.

Microsoft shut Skype down on May 5, 2025 and pointed everyone to Teams. Phaze is for people who wanted the messenger instead: a contact list, presence, mood messages and a simple chat window, now with encrypted DMs.

Import

How to import a Skype export

  1. Create your Phaze accountChoose a username and password and confirm your email address. A username close to the name friends saw in Skype makes it easier for their imports to find you.
  2. Find your Skype exportIt’s a .zip file you downloaded from go.skype.com/export, with a messages.json inside. Don’t unzip it.
  3. Upload itIn Phaze, open Settings → Chat History Import and choose the file. Exports up to 100 MB are accepted. Large ones take a minute.
  4. Find your peoplePhaze lists the contacts from your one-to-one Skype conversations. Add the ones already on Phaze, and send everyone else your invite link.

What the import brings over

  • Text messages, with who sent them and when. A conversation appears in your chat with that friend when their Phaze username matches the conversation’s name in Skype; the first 200 messages of each are shown today
  • Group chat text is saved to your account too, though Phaze doesn’t display imported group conversations yet
  • Contacts from your one-to-one conversations, matched to Phaze accounts by display name or username
  • An invite link for contacts who aren’t on Phaze yet
  • Photos, files and call recordings: the export only links to them on Skype’s servers, which no longer answer
  • Very long messages over 4,000 characters are skipped

What’s different

Better in places. Different in others.

Phaze isn’t a clone of Skype’s servers, so a few things work differently. Here they are up front.

DMs are end-to-end encrypted

Skype only encrypted “private conversations” you started on purpose. In Phaze, one-to-one DMs are encrypted automatically once both apps have exchanged keys. Group chats, Spaces and calls are not. Details.

No calls to phone numbers

There’s no Skype Credit, Skype Number or SMS. Calls are between Phaze users, and they’re free. Pricing.

Calls run on Jitsi Meet

Voice and video calls open a room on the public meet.jit.si service. They’re reliable across networks, but Jitsi’s servers carry the audio and video.

No phone number, no Microsoft account

You sign up with a username, password and email address. Phaze doesn’t read your address book or ask for your number.

FAQ

Switching from Skype

Is Phaze made by Microsoft or Skype?

No. Phaze is an independent, open-source project. It isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Microsoft or Skype. It recreates the feel of classic Skype with its own code, artwork and emoticons.

Can I still get my Skype export?

Microsoft offered exports at go.skype.com/export for a limited time after Skype closed, and has said that account data not exported would be deleted. If you saved the .zip back then, it’s worth checking your Downloads folder, an old laptop or a backup drive.

Will my Skype contacts be on Phaze automatically?

No. Each person needs their own Phaze account. The import lists the contacts from your Skype export and matches them to Phaze accounts with the same display name or username. Anyone it finds gets an Add friend button; for everyone else you can copy an invite link.

Can I keep my Skype Name?

Your Skype Name doesn’t transfer, but you can pick the same username on Phaze if nobody has taken it yet. The import lines old conversations up with Phaze usernames, so a username close to the name your friends saw in Skype helps them find you.

Does Phaze replace Skype Credit or Skype Number?

No. Phaze doesn’t connect to the phone network, so there’s no calling landlines or mobiles and no phone number to buy. Phaze-to-Phaze calls are free.

Is the imported history encrypted?

It’s stored on Phaze’s server like any other account data and protected in transit, but it isn’t end-to-end encrypted. New one-to-one DMs you send in Phaze are. Read the details.

Bring your contact list back.

Free, no phone number, nothing to install. Your Skype export is optional; your friends are not.
