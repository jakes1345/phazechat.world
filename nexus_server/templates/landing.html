Skype import

Bring your Skype history with you.

If you downloaded your Skype export before the service closed, that .zip still has years of conversations in it. Phaze can read it.

  1. Find your exportThe .zip file Skype gave you from go.skype.com/export, usually still in Downloads.
  2. Upload it in PhazeOpen Settings → Chat History Import and choose the file. Exports up to 100 MB are accepted.
  3. Reconnect with peoplePhaze lists your Skype contacts. Friends already on Phaze get an Add friend button; for the rest, copy your invite link.

What comes across

skype-export.zip messages.json inside your Skype export
  • Text messages, shown in your chat with each friend once you’ve added them
  • Your Skype contacts, matched to Phaze accounts where one exists
  • Photos and files: Skype’s file links no longer work, so they can’t be fetched

Imported history is stored on Phaze’s server and is not end-to-end encrypted.

What’s inside

Everything a messenger should do. Nothing it shouldn’t.

No feeds, no ads, no tutorial. Just your people, their status, and a chat window.

End-to-end encrypted DMs

Once your app and your friend’s have swapped keys, every DM is encrypted on your device with NaCl box. A lock in the message box shows when it’s on.

A real contact list

Friend requests, contact groups, blocking, and presence you can read at a glance: online, away, do not disturb. Plus the mood message under your name.

Voice & video calls

Call any friend from the chat header. Calls ring every device you’re signed in on and open in a Jitsi Meet room, on the web and on Android.

Six era themes

Phaze 1 through Phaze 6+ are modelled on how classic Skype looked from 2007 to 2018. The default, Phaze 5, is the 2014 Aero-blue look.

Group chats

Start a group from your contact list. The creator can add people, remove them and rename the chat. Group chats are not end-to-end encrypted, and the app says so.

Files & voice messages

Send photos, documents and archives up to 25 MB, or record a quick voice message on the web.

Spaces

Community servers with text channels, voice channels and a shared whiteboard. Available with the Phaze 6+ theme on the web.

Serious account security

Email verification, optional two-factor codes, approval for every new device, and a list of active sessions you can revoke.

Keep your keys

Back up your encryption keys behind a PIN only you know, so a new phone or browser can still read your encrypted history.

Privacy

Encryption, with the fine print printed large.

Lots of apps say “encrypted” and leave it there. Here is exactly what Phaze can and can’t read.

End-to-end encrypted
  • One-to-one DMs once both apps have exchanged keys. The server stores and relays only ciphertext.
  • Your key backup, encrypted on your device with a key derived from your PIN before it’s uploaded.
  • Key changes are caught: the web app pins each contact’s key and warns you if it ever changes.
Not end-to-end encrypted
  • DMs sent before keys are exchanged. No lock icon means the message goes as plain text.
  • Group chats, Spaces, stories and imported Skype history.
  • Calls, which run on Jitsi Meet, and attachment files themselves.
  • Metadata: the server knows who talks to whom, and when.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Is Phaze free?

Yes. Messages, calls, file sharing, Spaces and the Skype import cost nothing, and there are no ads. If you want to help with server costs you can chip in through Buy Me a Coffee; supporters get a badge on their profile and nothing else changes.

Can I import my Skype chats and contacts?

Yes, if you have the .zip export you downloaded from Skype. In Phaze, open Settings → Chat History Import and upload it (up to 100 MB). Phaze imports the text of your conversations, shows it in your chat with each friend, and lists your Skype contacts. Anyone who is already on Phaze gets an Add friend button; for everyone else you can copy an invite link. Photos and files can’t be brought over.

Is there an iPhone or iPad app?

Not a native one. Phaze runs in Safari: open phazechat.world/web, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, and it opens full-screen like an app. The same works on a Mac.

Are calls end-to-end encrypted?

No, and we’d rather say so up front. Voice and video calls open a room on the public Jitsi Meet service (meet.jit.si). The connection is encrypted in transit, but Jitsi’s servers handle the audio and video, so calls are not covered by Phaze’s end-to-end encryption. One-to-one DMs are. See exactly what is and isn’t encrypted.

Do I need a phone number?

No. You sign up with a username, a password and an email address, which you confirm with a code we send you. Phaze never asks for your phone number or your address book.

Can I call landlines and mobile numbers, like Skype Credit?

No. Phaze calls are between Phaze users only. There is no paid calling to the phone network.

Your contact list is waiting.

Pick a username, confirm your email, and you’re in. No phone number, no card, no download needed.
