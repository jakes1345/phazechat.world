Pricing

Phaze is free. Really.

No subscriptions, no credit packs, no premium tier, no ads. Everything below costs $0.

Messages & calls

$0

DMs, group chats, voice messages and voice and video calls between Phaze users.

Files

$0

Photos, audio, video, PDFs and archives, up to 25 MB each.

Skype import

$0

Bring your Skype export, up to 100 MB. How it works.

Self-hosting

$0

Open source under MIT. Run your own server from the Docker image.

No calls to phone numbers. Unlike Skype Credit, Phaze doesn’t connect to landlines or mobiles, and there are no phone numbers to buy. Calls are between Phaze users.

Supporters

How it stays free

phazechat.world runs on Cloudflare and is paid for by the project and by people who choose to chip in. Supporting is optional and never unlocks features: the chat stays free for everyone.

  • Donations go through Buy Me a Coffee. No payment details ever touch Phaze’s server.
  • Supporters get a supporter badge on their profile as a thank-you.
  • Inviting friends earns cosmetic reward crates. Nothing in Phaze is for sale.
