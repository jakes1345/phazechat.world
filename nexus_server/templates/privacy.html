Privacy policy

What Phaze stores about you, who else is involved in running it, and what you can do about it. Written to match what the code actually does.

Effective and last updated: November 6, 2026

Short version: one-to-one DMs are end-to-end encrypted once your apps have exchanged keys, and we can’t read those. We can read group chats, Spaces, stories, attachments and imported Skype history, and calls run on Jitsi Meet. We store your email and the IP addresses you sign up and sign in from. We don’t run ads, sell data or use analytics trackers.

1. Who we are

Phaze (“we”, “us”) is an independent, open-source messaging service at phazechat.world, with a web app, an Android app distributed as a direct download, and desktop apps for Windows and Linux. Phaze is not affiliated with Microsoft or Skype. For anything in this policy, email privacy@phazechat.world.

2. What we store

Your account

  • Username, email address and a bcrypt hash of your password (cost factor 12). Your email is used for verification, password resets, new-device approval codes and important account notices, never for marketing.
  • Optional profile details: display name, mood message, avatar and presence status.
  • Your friends list, contact groups and block list, Spaces you’ve joined, and your app settings.
  • If you turn them on: your two-factor secret and backup codes, and an encrypted key backup (ciphertext, salt and iteration count only).
  • If you link one: a phone number, which is verified by SMS. Phaze never requires this.

Devices, sign-ins and IP addresses

  • The IP address you signed up from and the IP address of your most recent sign-in, stored on your account for abuse prevention and moderation. These are kept for as long as your account exists.
  • A record of each device you’ve approved: a label derived from your browser or device (for example “Chrome on Windows”), when it was last seen and its last IP address.
  • Session tokens for signed-in devices, until they expire or you sign out or revoke them.
  • Push notification tokens: a Web Push subscription for each browser and a Firebase Cloud Messaging token for Android.

We do not store your public encryption key. Apps exchange public keys directly through the server when a chat is opened, and the server only forwards them between friends.

3. Messages, calls and files

  • Direct messages are stored on our server so your history follows you between devices. When both apps have exchanged keys, a DM is encrypted on your device with NaCl box (X25519, XSalsa20-Poly1305) and we store only the ciphertext. A DM sent before that, shown without a lock icon in the web app, is stored as plain text. We also store who sent each message, to whom and when. Deleting a DM erases its content; a record that a message existed remains.
  • Group chats, Spaces channel messages and stories are stored in readable form. They are not end-to-end encrypted, and the server and its administrators can read them.
  • Attachments are uploaded and stored unencrypted under a random file name. Anyone with the link can download the file. Attachments are not automatically deleted.
  • Stories are hidden and their database record removed 24 hours after posting, or when you delete them. The photo or video file itself currently stays in storage.
  • Imported Skype history: if you upload a Skype export, we store the text messages and contact names from it, readable, with your account.
  • Calls and voice channels run in a room on the public Jitsi Meet service (see section 5). We keep a call log of who called whom, the call type and when. We never record call audio or video.
  • Live streams and remote screen sessions connect browsers directly over WebRTC. When a direct connection isn’t possible, encrypted media is relayed through our TURN server, which can’t decrypt it.
  • Abuse reports you file, and reports filed about you, including the reason and any message text included in the report.

4. What we don’t do

  • No advertising, ad identifiers or tracking pixels.
  • No third-party analytics. We don’t sell, rent or share your data for anyone’s marketing.
  • We don’t upload your address book or ask for your phone number.
  • We don’t collect your precise location.
  • We don’t use your messages to train machine-learning models.

5. Third-party services

Running Phaze involves the services below. Some are only active when the operator has configured them, which is noted.

  • Cloudflare (hosting and storage). Phaze’s server runs on Cloudflare’s infrastructure (we are moving it to Cloudflare Containers), and web traffic passes through Cloudflare’s network, which sees your IP address and request details. Database backups and uploaded files are stored in Cloudflare R2. Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Jitsi Meet (8x8, Inc.). Voice and video calls and Spaces voice channels open a room on meet.jit.si. Jitsi’s servers see your IP address, the room and your call’s audio and video. Jitsi Meet privacy policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google). Delivers push notifications to the Android app. Google receives your device’s push token and the notification. Google privacy policy.
  • Browser push services. Web push notifications are delivered by your browser maker’s push service (for example Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft). Notification content is encrypted for your browser under the Web Push standard; the push service sees the subscription and delivery timing.
  • Email delivery: Resend or Brevo, whichever is configured, or a plain SMTP provider. They receive your email address and the content of the emails we send (verification codes, password-reset links, device-approval codes, supporter thank-yous).
  • Twilio (only if you link a phone number). Receives your phone number and the verification code.
  • Google Gemini and Anthropic (only when configured). The in-app support assistant sends what you type to it, along with the conversation so far, to whichever of the two is configured. The optional Nova bot account sends messages you write to Nova, and group or channel messages that mention @nova, to Google Gemini. Nothing else is sent to them.
  • Sentry (only when configured). Error reports from the server and apps, which can include your IP address, browser or device details and what the app was doing when it failed.
  • proxycheck.io and ip-api.com (only when VPN blocking is turned on). The IP address of a new sign-up is checked to see whether it belongs to a VPN or data centre. Administrators can also look up the approximate location of an account’s IP address through ip-api.com when investigating abuse.
  • Buy Me a Coffee (only if you donate). Payments are handled entirely by Buy Me a Coffee; it tells us the supporter’s name, email address and amount so we can add the supporter badge. If you use the supporter form we store your username, name and email.
  • Google Fonts and GitHub (website only). These marketing pages load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, and the download page asks GitHub for the latest release, so your browser contacts those services and they see your IP address. Desktop downloads are served from GitHub.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app under Settings → Danger. This immediately removes your account, friends, direct messages, Space memberships and messages, stories, settings, push subscriptions, key backup, two-factor codes and sessions. Items marked above as not removed automatically (such as uploaded files, imported Skype history and device records) can be purged on request: email privacy@phazechat.world.
  • Export your data from Settings, or through /api/v1/export. The export currently contains your profile, friends list and messages still waiting to be delivered. For a fuller copy of what we hold, email us.
  • Correct your data: change your display name, mood and avatar in Settings, or email us.
  • Object or ask questions: email privacy@phazechat.world. We aim to answer within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR), you may also have the right to restrict processing or complain to your data protection authority.

8. Children

Phaze isn’t meant for children under 13, or under 16 where local law requires it. If you believe a child has made an account, email privacy@phazechat.world and we’ll delete it.

9. Legal requests

We respond to valid legal process. We can’t hand over the content of end-to-end encrypted DMs because we don’t have the keys. We can produce what this policy says we store, including account details, IP addresses, metadata and any content that isn’t end-to-end encrypted.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we’ll update the date at the top and mention it in the app. The full history of this page is public in the source repository.

11. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: privacy@phazechat.world. Security issues: see Security.
