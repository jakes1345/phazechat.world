Support

How can we help?

Quick answers for the common questions, and a direct line to the people who build Phaze for everything else.

Signing up and signing in

You need a username, a password and an email address. We email a code to verify it, and again when you sign in on a new device unless you approve it from one that’s already signed in. Forgot your password? Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen.

Importing from Skype

Settings → Chat History Import accepts the .zip from Skype, up to 100 MB. Text imports; photos and files can’t. Step-by-step guide.

Calls and video

Calls open in a Jitsi Meet room, so allow camera and microphone access when your browser asks. If a call won’t connect, try another browser or network and tell us what happened.

New device and recovery PIN

Set a recovery PIN in Settings → Backup & Devices so a new browser or phone can restore your encryption keys and keep reading encrypted history.

Installing Phaze

Use the web app anywhere, install the Android APK, or try the Windows and Linux desktop beta. iPhone, iPad and Mac use the web app. Downloads.

Security and privacy

What’s encrypted, what isn’t, and how to report a vulnerability privately. Security · Privacy policy.

Export or delete your data

Download an export or delete your account from Settings in the web app. For anything the in-app tools don’t cover, email privacy@phazechat.world.

Report a bug

Something broken? Open an issue on GitHub with what you did, what you expected and which app you were using. Open an issue.

Is Phaze down?

The status page shows the server’s health checks for the last 24 hours. Service status.
